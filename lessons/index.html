Questions

What exactly do I get?

One folder per pack: interactive slides (open in any browser), touchscreen games that work offline, printables (PDF), and a step-by-step teacher guide. Nothing to install and nothing to log into.

What level are the lessons for?

Low-level learners, roughly pre-A1 to A1. Sentences are short, pictures carry the meaning, and every game reinforces the same few words so everyone keeps up.

Do the lessons work offline?

Yes. Open the slides or games once while you have internet; from then on they load from your device, so they keep working through dropped school Wi-Fi.

Can I try before paying?

Yes — every pack has a demo link where you can play the games free. If the level or style doesn’t suit your class, no money spent.

How long is each lesson?

About 40 minutes as written, and easy to stretch or shorten. The teacher guide suggests where to trim if you have less time, or where to add practice if you have more.

Can I use it on a smartboard?

That’s what it’s made for. Big pictures, big buttons, and games built for tapping and dragging on a touchscreen — including Seewo boards.
