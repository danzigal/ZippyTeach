How to Use OCM Board

A quick guide for teachers — downloading it, setting up your first class, and keeping it updated.

Getting started

Download and open the app.

Download the trial or full version

Get the file from the OCM Board page — the free trial runs for 3 days with everything unlocked except a few features. Once you're ready to buy, you'll receive your own permanently-unlocked copy by email.

Open the file — no installation needed

Just double-click the downloaded file and it opens directly in your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari all work). There's nothing to install, no account to create, and no internet connection required to run it.

Set up your first class

From "My Classes," paste your student roster straight into the seating chart, or type names in one at a time. Pick a seating layout — Flexible, U-Shape, Pair Seating, or Clusters/Pods — and drag any desk to fine-tune it.

Everything saves automatically

Every change — points, seating, rules, flashcards — saves itself in your browser as you go. Nothing to click, nothing to remember.

💾 Still worth doing a backup now and then — see "Backing up your data" below.

Keeping it updated

How to update to a new version.

From time to time you'll get an updated file with new features or fixes. Follow these steps so your saved classes, points, and rules carry over safely.

Back up your current data first

Open your current app, go to My Classes, open "💾 Backup / restore this computer's data," and click "⬇️ Download Backup." This saves a small file with everything — keep it somewhere safe just in case.

Download the new version

Save the new file in the exact same folder as your old one, using the exact same file name. This is what lets your browser recognize it as the same app and keep your saved data automatically.

Open the new file and check your data

Your classes, points, rules, and flashcards should all still be there, right where you left them.

If anything's missing, restore your backup

Go to My Classes → Backup → "⬆️ Load Backup," and select the file you downloaded in step 1. This restores everything exactly as it was.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need an internet connection to use this?

No. Once you've downloaded the file, it runs completely offline — no VPN, no internet connection needed at all.

Will I lose my data if I close the browser?

No — everything saves automatically to your browser as you use the app. It'll be there next time you open the same file from the same location.

Can two teachers share the same class?

The app doesn't sync between devices automatically, since it works entirely offline. If you co-teach a class, use "Download Backup" after your session and "Load Backup" on the other device to bring your data across.

I see a yellow or red banner about storage — what do I do?

This means your saved data is getting large, almost always from lots of Student Profile notes built up over time. Go to My Classes → Backup → "🗄️ Archive Old Profile Notes" to export and clear older entries — nothing is lost, it's just moved to a backup file.

What happens when my free trial ends?

After 3 days, the trial locks itself and shows a screen with purchase details. Your existing classes and data are kept — once you switch to the full version, everything you already set up carries over.
