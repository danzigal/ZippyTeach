Questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No account. You need internet the first time you open the board. After that, it works offline and updates itself when you’re back online.

Where are my classes saved?

Only on your own device. Use Download Backup in the board every week or two to keep a copy, and to move classes to another computer.

What student information should I put in?

First names or nicknames only. The board is your own classroom notebook, not an official school record. Keep official reports in your school’s system. Privacy policy

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Enter your name and code once on each computer. With the backup, you can bring just the classes you need onto each classroom computer.

Is there a guide?

Yes. Tap How to use at the top of the board for a short guide to every feature.
