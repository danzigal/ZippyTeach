Questions

What do I need to use it?

PowerPoint for the slides, and Edge or Chrome for the games. It works on Seewo and other smartboards, laptops and tablets. The games don’t need internet.

Does it follow my textbook?

It pairs with the PEP “My friends” unit, and works with any course that teaches describing people. The pack is independent and uses its own characters.

What level is it for?

Low English: Pre-A1 to A1. Everything is picture-led with sentence frames and word banks. For very low classes the guide shows how to split it over two lessons and make a shorter book.

Can I share it with colleagues?

One pack is for one teacher and their own classes. Colleagues can buy their own copy, and school licences are available.

How do the games open from the slides?

Keep the slides and the games file in the same folder. The last slide has an “Open the games” button.
