Privacy Policy – ZippyTeach Ocean Board

Effective date: 11 September 2026

ZippyTeach Ocean Board is a classroom management app made by Daniella Da Silva (ZippyTeach) for teachers. This policy explains how the app handles information.

We do not collect personal information

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Information saved on your device

Your settings and anything you enter into the board (for example, names or scores) are saved only on your own device. This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Microphone

Some features use the microphone to respond to sound in the classroom. Sound is processed on the device in real time only. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. You can allow or deny microphone access in your device settings at any time.

Printing

When you print from the app, your device's built-in print system is used. What you print goes only to the printer or PDF you choose. The app does not keep a copy.

Children

The app is designed for teachers to use in the classroom. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
